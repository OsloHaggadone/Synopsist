        About

          Privacy

          What this site records. While you listen, the player notes when you play, pause, skip, or finish a song, or jump to another moment in it. Each note holds the song, the moment, and the time. The notes are kept in a database for reference.

          Why. It shows the artist which parts of each song listeners replay, skip, or stop at, so they can better understand their audience. It also lets the site recommend songs from the moments you replay, rather than from genre.

          What it doesn't record. No name, email, account, or location, and no advertising or third-party trackers. Your listening is linked only to a random ID created in this browser, never to your IP address.

          You can turn this off at any time. The player works the same either way, and turning it off also deletes the random ID from this browser, and any notes still waiting to be sent.

          Get the app

          Add Synopsist to your iPhone's Home Screen and it opens like an app: full screen, with the lock screen's controls, and it updates by itself. Free, with no App Store and no account.

          You're in the app already.

          On iPhone or iPad

          First open this page in Safari: the browsers inside apps like Instagram and TikTok can't add to the Home Screen. Tap and choose to open it in your browser.

          1. In Safari, open oslohaggadone.github.io/Synopsist.
          2. Tap next to the address, then Share . On an iPhone before iOS 26, tap Share at the bottom of the screen.
          3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
          4. Leave Open as Web App on, and tap Add.
          5. Open Synopsist from your Home Screen.

          On Android or a computer

          In Chrome or Edge, open the browser's menu and choose Install (or Add to Home screen).

          The app shares anonymous listening data the way the website does, and Settings can turn it off. Read the privacy note
