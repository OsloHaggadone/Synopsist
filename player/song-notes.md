# Song notes

These are the summaries shown at the top of each song's details panel on the website. They're first
drafts, written from the audio analysis (the moods, instruments, and shape it found in each song)
rather than from listening, so some of them may miss the mark. Write your own over any of them.

How it works: each song starts with a line beginning "## " and the song's track id from
data/catalog.csv (the title after it is only a reminder). Everything below that line, up to the
next "## ", is the note. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Save the file and refresh the page
to see the change; there's nothing to rebuild. A song without a note gets an automatic description
made from its analysis.


## eternal-synopsis · Eternal Synopsis

A bright, high-energy rush that wastes no time. After a few seconds of build-up it locks into a
dense, full wall of sound and holds it all the way through, with driving drums and heavy bass under
a soaring melodic lead and what sounds like a voice woven in. Epic and spacey, with a touch of
dubstep weight.


## hollow-journey-beyond · Hollow Journey Beyond

A slow-burning, shadowy journey that takes its time. For the first two minutes it drifts through a
dim, half-lit space, sounds rising and falling like distant signals, until the bass finally arrives
around 2:12 and the song opens up into a wide, dense swell that carries it to the end. The harmony
barely moves, which gives it the feeling of floating in place in the dark.

## volition · Volition

Volition starts from near silence and settles into a steady, confident groove at an easy walking
pace. Once the bass comes in around 0:56, the layers stack up quickly: synths, guitar, piano, and
drums fill out a warm, downtempo sound that feels spacey and determined at the same time. It keeps
that momentum almost to the end, easing back only for the closing stretch.

## crimson-eclipse · Crimson Eclipse

A bright, upbeat track that grows in steps. It opens almost silently, the bass kicks in around
0:20, and from there each section feels a little bigger and more confident than the last, peaking
near 1:48. For all that energy, the sound stays clean and uncluttered: a deep, heavy low end with
plenty of space above it.

## impulse · Impulse

Short, punchy, and made to move to. Impulse is a disco-tinged dance cut with a busy, bouncing rhythm
and a melody that leaps all over the place, bright and playful from the first second. It lifts to
its fullest around 0:48, then eases back for the last stretch.

## flowers-about · Flowers About

A warm, easygoing groove built around a deep, round bass line. It circles just a couple of chords the
whole way, letting the drums, keys, and little percussion details do the talking, and after a short
dip around 1:20 it comes back noticeably fuller. Relaxed and sunny, like a chill-out track that
can't quite sit still.

## planetary-fields-2024-remastered-version · Planetary Fields (2024 Remastered Version)

A long, open-hearted journey of more than seven minutes. Piano and soft synths set a calm, hopeful
mood, and the song slowly gathers drums, guitars, and more as it goes, never in a hurry. Around 4:16
everything thins out for a breather before it rises once more, then it fades away gently, like the
sun going down over a wide field.

## jiggly-cake · Jiggly Cake

Pure fun. Jiggly Cake is a bouncy, summery house track full of playful little hooks, with synths,
bass, and drums trading ideas at a quick, dance-ready pace. The chords and melody keep changing, so
there's always something new popping up, and a short break around 1:20 sets up a big return at 1:32.

## glistening-dawn · Glistening Dawn

A soft, jazzy morning piece. Warm keys and bass move through a lot of chords for such a gentle song,
and the melody wanders freely over the top, giving it the relaxed, slightly romantic feel of a
lounge set at sunrise. The sound stays mellow and round, with almost no bright edges, and pauses
briefly around 1:48 before the last stretch.

## you-know-what-that-means · You know what that means... !

A one-minute build and release. It starts small and keeps teasing for half a minute, then the bass
lands around 0:28, and by 0:44 it's an all-out celebration: bright, bouncy, and party-ready right up
to the last second.

## facades · Facades

Facades unfolds slowly, like something being revealed piece by piece. A hushed, atmospheric opening,
with one early flare, gives way to a gradual build. The bass arrives around 1:24, and after a short
break near 2:00 the song spreads into its widest, most layered stretch, peaking around 3:52 before
it fades out. Spacey and melodic, with a darker undercurrent.

## journey · Journey

A compact, driving song that finds one level and stays there. Thick, wall-to-wall synths and guitar
keep the sound dense from start to finish, with a strong, voice-like lead riding on top, and the
steady, danceable pulse never lets up. Spacey but energetic, like cruising at full speed.

## dying-light · Dying Light

Dense, glowing, and always moving forward. Dying Light packs a lot in: layered synths, piano, and
guitar, a melody that climbs and dives across a wide range, and chords that keep shifting, all held
at one steady, intense level. A brief dip around 2:45 sets up the final push, which peaks around
3:12. Bright on the surface, a little bittersweet underneath.

## planetary-fields-final-ver · Planetary Fields (Final Ver.)

The full, seven-and-a-half-minute version of Planetary Fields, and one of the most spacious songs
here. Piano leads a calm, hopeful walk that slowly picks up drums, bass, and guitars, while the sound
itself stays airy and uncluttered. A long, quiet valley around 4:16 gives way to its fullest moment
near 5:28, and then it drifts out softly.

## planetary-fields-milestone · Planetary Fields (Milestone)

An earlier milestone of Planetary Fields, shorter and simpler than the final version. It finds the
same calm, hopeful mood with just a handful of chords and a gentle, close-knit melody, building
patiently from a hesitant start to a warm plateau, then ending quite suddenly, like a sketch of the
bigger journey to come.

## hollow-journey · Hollow Journey

The opening track of the Hollow Journey soundtrack: quiet, patient, and dreamlike. It begins almost
in a whisper, the bass slips in around 0:24, and the music slowly swells into a fuller, brighter
second half, with some of the biggest swings between soft and loud of any song here. A meditative
start to the journey.

## electric-space · Electric Space

A delicate, almost classical miniature. With virtually no bass at all, it lives entirely in the
middle of the sound: piano and soft synth tones repeating in gentle, rippling patterns, emotional and
quietly uplifting, like a short scene from a film score.

## into-space · Into Space

A calm, steady drift outward. Piano and synths keep things simple and spacious, the bass joins
around 0:32, and a short pause near 1:16 resets the mood before the song climbs to its fullest point
around 2:24. Relaxed, slightly dark, and never crowded.

## space-rain · Space Rain

A hypnotic, slowly falling curtain of sound. Space Rain stays on nearly the same chord and the same
few notes for four minutes, letting a heavy, warm low end and gently shifting layers of piano,
guitar, and drums wash over each other at one even level. It's less about where it goes than about
the feeling of standing in it.

## zero-gravity · Zero Gravity

Weightless and gently melodic. After a quiet opening, the bass floats in around 0:24 and the song
settles into a laid-back, drifting groove, with chords that keep sliding somewhere new. It pauses for
a moment of stillness around 2:16, then rises to its fullest near 3:12 before floating away.

## negative-space · Negative Space

An ambient soundscape with no beat to hold on to. Soft synth and piano tones hang in empty space,
swelling from near silence into a glowing middle section and then dissolving again. Meditative and
airy, it lives up to its name: the silence around the notes matters as much as the notes.

## tropical-space-day · Tropical Space (Day)

A dreamy daytime cruise through space. Synths and a drum machine carry a bouncy, melodic line, the
bass drops in around 0:16, and the track quickly opens up into its full groove before settling into
a softer final stretch.

## tropical-space-day-extended · Tropical Space (Day) - Extended

The longer cut of Tropical Space (Day), with more room to roam. The same dreamy, melodic synth line
rides a light drum-machine groove, and the extended form adds a softer interlude around 1:36 before
the full groove returns for a second, longer run.

## bad-boy · Bad Boy

Short, dark, and full of attitude. Bad Boy opens with a punch, cuts to near silence for a moment of
suspense, then drops the bass around 0:24 and rides a deep, techno-tinged groove all the way to the
end. Sparse in sound but big on contrast.

## spacial-awareness · Spacial Awareness

A long, unhurried drift with plenty of room to breathe. Synths, piano, and guitar settle into a slow,
dreamy groove over just a few chords, and the song is happy to stay there. Around 2:48 the bass
steps away for a quiet stretch, then returns and carries the song to its fullest point near 4:20
before it fades.

## delta-gravity-delta-side · Delta Gravity (Delta-Side)

A bright, flowing piece with a melody that likes to roam. Over a steady, gently danceable beat,
synths, piano, and guitar weave long melodic lines that climb and fall across a wide range, building
toward a high point around 2:16 and keeping that warm, hopeful glow for the rest of the song.

## hollow-truth · Hollow Truth

The most layered song in the collection so far, and the most cinematic. Hollow Truth starts in near
darkness, gathers itself as the bass comes in at 0:32, falls back into an uneasy quiet at 1:00, and
then builds into a sweeping, orchestral climax around 2:08, with strings, piano, synths, and drums
all at full strength. Epic and dramatic, like the turning point of a story.

## ephemeral-synopsis · Ephemeral Synopsis

A dreamy, smoothly rolling track that keeps evolving under a calm surface. The loudness barely
changes, but the chords keep shifting and the melody covers a remarkably wide range. The bass joins
at 0:32, the song reaches its fullest around 2:04, and it eases out gently at the end.

## absolute-zero · Absolute Zero

Cold, still, and quietly dramatic. Built from little more than synths and piano, it creeps in from a
hushed start, warms into a glowing, full-bodied middle as the bass arrives around 0:32, then drops
back to near silence for its final seconds, like a breath held in the cold.

## bells · Bells

The simplest song here, and maybe the most peaceful: under a minute of soft, chiming tones that swell
and fade in slow waves. Meditative and a little melancholy, it feels like a moment of stillness
between bigger journeys.

## eating-the-suspicious-green-cookie · Eating the Suspicious Green Cookie

A woozy, dreamlike trip that makes good on its title. A drum machine and bass keep a relaxed, steady
pace while the synths slide through chord after chord, with more harmonic twists than almost any
other song here, so the ground never quite stops shifting. Spacey, a little dark, and pleasantly
disorienting.

## out-of-space · Out of Space

A laid-back, dreamy downtempo groove. Synths, piano, and soft drums move at an easy pace, with a
melody that floats freely over shifting chords. A sudden hush around 0:48 resets everything, and the
song comes back fuller and warmer for its second half.

## suspicious-green-cookie · Suspicious Green Cookie

A relaxed, lounge-style snack of a song. Deep bass and a drum machine lay down a smooth, unhurried
groove with very little on top, while the chords quietly keep changing underneath. Sparse, cool, and
over before you know it.

## nai · nai

A bright, melodic little synthpop piece with a video-game charm. It starts light and playful, the
bass comes in around 0:24, and the second half blooms into a fuller, warmer sound, with chords that
keep changing to hold your interest.
